Colorful Birds

1.mandarin duck

The mandarin duck is a beautiful bird native to East Asia, known for its colorful appearance. First, its appearance: males have bright feathers in shades of orange, purple, green, and red, while females have more subtle grayish-brown feathers. Second, its behavior: mandarin ducks are usually shy and prefer to stay in small groups near trees and water. Third, its diet: they eat a variety of foods, including seeds, acorns, insects, snails, and small fish. (Wikipedia)

2.common kingfisher

The common kingfisher is a small, colorful bird commonly found near rivers, lakes, and streams. First, its appearance: it has bright blue and turquoise feathers on its back, orange feathers on its underside, and a long, pointed beak. Second, its behavior: it often sits quietly on branches above the water before diving quickly to catch its prey. Third, its diet: the common kingfisher mainly eats small fish, but it also feeds on aquatic insects and other small water animals. (Wikipedia)

3.lilac-brasted-roller

The lilac-breasted roller is a colorful bird native to sub-Saharan Africa, known for its bright feathers. First, its appearance: it has a lilac-colored breast, a turquoise belly, and blue wings with dark edges. Second, its behavior: it often sits on tree branches or other high spots and performs impressive rolling dives during courtship or territorial displays. Third, its diet: it mainly eats insects, such as grasshoppers and beetles, but it also feeds on small lizards and other small animals. (Wikipedia)
